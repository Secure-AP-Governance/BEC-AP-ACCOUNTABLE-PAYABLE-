# Infrastructure Team

## Purpose
Verify runtime, network, secrets, and deployment prerequisites.

## Scope
- Runtime requirements
- Secrets handling
- TLS and networking
- Resource limits
- Backup/recovery
- Deployment health

## Responsibilities
- Confirm infrastructure requirements are documented and valid.
- Verify secrets handling is compliant.
- Confirm deployment and rollback procedures are defined.

## Required evidence
- Runtime and network requirements
- Secrets and TLS evidence
- Backup/recovery verification

## Approval criteria
- Production environment can be validated.
- Secrets are handled securely.
- Recovery/rollback process is defined.

## Rejection criteria
- Production infrastructure evidence is missing.
- Secrets or credentials are exposed.
